A smart TV is not just a screen; it is an account device, a microphone-capable remote in some homes, a streaming app hub, and a network endpoint that may stay logged in for years. This guide was checked on 2026-06-17 against FTC, CISA, NIST, FCC, Consumer Reports, and platform privacy resources. It is not a forensic or legal guide; if a device is part of stalking, harassment, workplace monitoring, or a breach investigation, preserve evidence and ask a qualified professional before resetting it.

## Fast decision table

| Situation | Safer choice | Mistake to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| New TV/app setup | Use separate account and minimal permissions | Signing into every app by habit |
| Voice or viewing data setting | Review privacy menu and disable what you do not need | Assuming defaults are private |
| Old TV being sold | Sign out, factory reset, remove device from accounts | Handing over a logged-in screen |
| Suspicious account activity | Change passwords and secure accounts before reset | Deleting evidence too early |
